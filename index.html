FAQ

Questions?
Answered.

Everything you need to know before we start. Can't find your answer? Reach out — we reply within a few hours.

Ask a question →

How long does it take to build a website?

+
Every project is different, but most websites are completed within 1–4 weeks, depending on the size and complexity. Before we begin, we'll provide a clear timeline so you always know what to expect.

How much does a website cost?

+
Our pricing depends on the type of website you need. Simple websites start from ₦20,000, while larger business websites and custom projects are priced based on their requirements. Contact us for a free quote with no obligation.

Do you redesign existing websites?

+
Yes! If your current website feels outdated, loads slowly, or no longer reflects your business, we can redesign it with a modern look, better performance, and an improved user experience.

Will my website work on phones and tablets?

+
Absolutely. Every website we build is fully responsive, meaning it works beautifully on desktops, tablets, and smartphones.

Can I update my website myself?

+
Yes. Depending on your project, we can build your website so it's easy to update text, images, blog posts, and other content without needing technical knowledge.

Do you provide hosting and domain setup?

+
Yes. We can help you purchase a domain name, set up reliable hosting, connect your website, and make sure everything is configured correctly.

Do you offer website maintenance?

+
Yes. We provide ongoing maintenance, including content updates, bug fixes, security checks, and performance improvements so your website stays in great condition.

Can you build an online store?

+
Yes. We can create e-commerce websites that allow customers to browse products, make secure payments, and manage orders efficiently.

Will my website appear on Google?

+
Yes. Every website is built with SEO best practices such as clean code, fast loading speeds, proper page structure, and metadata to help search engines understand your site. Ranking well also depends on your content and competition.

What information do you need before starting?

+
Usually we'll ask for: Your business name, what your business does, your goals, branding (if available), images and content, and any websites you like for inspiration. If you don't have everything ready, don't worry—we'll guide you through the process.

What happens after my website is launched?

+
After launch, we'll test everything one final time and make sure your website is running smoothly. If you choose a maintenance plan, we'll continue to keep it updated and assist whenever needed.

Why should I choose your agency?

+
We focus on creating websites that are fast, modern, responsive, and designed around your business goals. Every project is built with attention to detail, clear communication, and a commitment to quality from start to finish.

Do you use templates?

+
No. Every website is designed around your business and goals. We don't simply install a generic template—we create a website tailored to your brand.

Can I request changes during the project?

+
Of course. We encourage collaboration throughout the project, and you'll have opportunities to review the work and request revisions before launch.

Do you only work with businesses in Nigeria?

+
No. We work remotely with clients from anywhere in the world.

What if I'm not sure what I need?

+
That's completely okay. We'll discuss your business, goals, and budget during a free consultation and recommend the best solution for you.

Can you improve my website's speed?

+
Yes. We optimize every website for fast loading, improved user experience, and better search engine performance.
