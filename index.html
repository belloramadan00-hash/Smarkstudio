07 — FAQ

Common questions.

Still have a question? Contact us.

What services does SMARK Studio offer?

SMARK Studio helps businesses build a professional online presence through websites, promotional flyers, AI-powered videos, and product design (UI/UX).

How much do your services cost?

Pricing depends on the specific scope and package. We offer clear starting prices for our packages. For custom projects, contact us for a tailored quote with no obligation.

How long does a project take?

Timelines vary based on the service. Websites typically take 1-4 weeks, while flyers and AI videos can be delivered faster. We provide a clear timeline before starting.

Can I combine multiple services?

Yes! Our "Brand Growth" and "Complete Digital Brand" packages are designed specifically for businesses that want a cohesive online presence across multiple formats.

Can you redesign an existing website or design?

Yes. If your current website or promotional materials feel outdated, we can redesign them with a modern look, better performance, and an improved user experience.

How do payments, revisions and delivery work?

We typically require a deposit to begin. The number of revisions and the final delivery format are agreed upon in the project scope before we start.

How do I get started?

Choose a package or contact us for a quote. We'll discuss your business, goals, and what you need before starting.
